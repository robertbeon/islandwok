Privacy Policy

Central Ocean Kreatur

At Central Ocean Creatur Aquarium, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, purchase tickets, or participate in our programs and events.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information: Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing details provided when you register, make a purchase, or sign up for events. Technical Information: Browser type, device data, IP address, and usage patterns gathered through cookies and analytics tools to improve your website experience. Interaction Data: Photos, feedback, or media you share during events, surveys, or promotional activities.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us to: Process bookings, payments, and customer support requests. Send important updates, event information, and promotional offers (only if you have opted in). Improve our programs, website experience, and visitor safety. Maintain compliance with legal or safety requirements.

3. How We Protect Your Data

We use secure servers, encryption, and access controls to protect your personal data. Only authorized staff members have access to customer information for legitimate business purposes. While we strive to maintain top-level security, no online platform is completely risk-free. By using our site, you acknowledge and accept these inherent risks.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share limited data only in the following cases: With trusted service providers who assist in payment processing, ticketing, or email communication. When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our visitors, animals, and staff. With your consent, for promotional or collaborative events.

5. Use of Cookies

Our website uses cookies to personalize your experience and analyze site performance. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies; however, some features of the site may not function properly without them.

6. Photography and Media Policy

By attending our events, you agree that photos or videos taken during your visit may be used for educational, promotional, or social media purposes by the Central Ocean Creatur Aquarium, unless you notify us in writing prior to participation.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: Request access to your personal information. Correct or update your data. Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.  To make a request, please contact our Privacy Office at privacy@centraloceanaquarium.com.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date.” Please review periodically for the latest information.

9. Contact Us

For any questions, concerns, or data requests, please contact: Central Ocean Creatur Aquarium Email: privacy@centraloceanaquarium.com Phone: (000) 123-4567 Address: 123 Ocean Drive, Marina City, CA
