Visitor Tips

We want every visit to Central Ocean Kreatur to be a wonderful and memorable experience for both our guests and our marine friends! Here are a few friendly reminders to help make your visit special:

  • Show kindness to our ocean creatures. Watching them calmly helps them feel safe and happy no tapping on the glass, please!
  • Capture the magic! Photography is welcome just remember to turn off your flash to protect sensitive sea life.
  • Relax and enjoy. Take a break at The Coral Café, where you can enjoy ocean views and refreshing snacks.
  • Bring home a memory. Visit our Gift Shop for unique souvenirs that directly support ocean conservation.
  • Help us stay clean and green. Use recycling bins and keep our spaces tidy so everyone including our marine residents can enjoy a healthy environment.

Every smile, photo, and small act of care helps us keep Central Ocean Kreatur a safe, joyful home for all our kreatur of the sea!
